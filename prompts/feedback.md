# Student feedback drafting prompt

Given a completed draft grading report and instructor comments, draft concise, respectful, specific feedback:

- What the student did well (cite artifacts)
- Two or three high-impact changes
- Exactly what to revise/submit next, with deadlines only if approved
- A positive closing that does not promise certification

Only produce a **draft**. Instructor approval is mandatory before sending.

Subject template: `[Elevate Bootcamp] Week {week} Assignment Feedback — {track}`
