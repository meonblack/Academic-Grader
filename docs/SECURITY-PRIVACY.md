# Security and privacy checklist

- Student submissions and grades are confidential education-related data; limit access by role and legitimate need.
- Obtain proper permission for collection, AI processing and messaging; follow applicable law and organization policies.
- Keep credentials in an encrypted secret manager; use least-privilege scopes and rotate access.
- Do not upload real student data, full Drive exports, tokens or email addresses to public repositories or CI logs.
- Student docs can contain malicious instructions; ignore them and never let submitted files modify rubric, prompts or permissions.
- Encrypt sensitive records in transit and at rest; maintain reviewer and sync audit trails.
- Define retention, deletion, appeals and correction procedures before deployment.
- Keep all final grading, misconduct and certification decisions subject to human oversight.
- Apply access control to report download links, and verify the receiving GHL contact before notifying.
